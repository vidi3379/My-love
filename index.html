My Love,

Kabhi-kabhi sochti hoon ki hamari story actually kab shuru hui thi... Shayad us din jab main pehli baar tumhare ghar aayi thi aur hum mile the. Tab mujhe bilkul idea nahi tha ki ek normal si meeting meri life ki itni beautiful memories ban jayegi.

Hum pehli baar mile, aur kuch hi din baad tumne mere liye woh trip plan ki. Aur phir aayi woh trip — hamari story ka sabse special chapter. ❤️

Raat ko coincidence se hamari seats same thi, aur pata nahi kab main tumhare shoulder par sir rakh kar so gayi. Itna simple moment tha, lekin aaj bhi mere dil ke bahut close hai.

Ek hi scooty par hum dono, tum ride kar rahe the aur main peeche baithi thi. Har beautiful place par rukna, saath enjoy karna aur meri endless bak-bak sunna. 😂

Woh side wali jagah jahan humne itni saari baatein ki, Dhari Devi Mandir ke paas tumhe cigarette ke liye tokna aur tumhara meri baat maan lena... sab yaad hai mujhe.

Us raat bike par tumhe tightly pakad kar sona, hotel mein paas-paas ek bed par sona, aur agle din tumhara mujhe jacket dena... these little moments became my favourite memories.

Phir saath mein trek karna aur trek ke beech mera tumse baar-baar relationship ko lekar questions poochna. 😂

And then came our special day...

5 April 2026 ❤️

The day we officially became US. Saath mein khana khana, wapas aana, raste mein enjoy karna... suddenly everything felt even more special.

Tumhara mujhe tease karna, jealous karna, meri care karna, meri pampering karna aur mere saare nakhre handle karna. 😂

Tumhara Delhi se Gurgaon aana, jab-jab maine bulaya tab-tab mere paas aana... I don't think you realise how much these little things mean to me.

Yes, we fought. We had misunderstandings. Kabhi maine breakup tak ki baat kar di. But our story isn't about being perfect. It's about learning, understanding and choosing each other even after difficult days.

I love your care, your smile, your teasing, your little efforts and even the way you irritate me. 😂❤️

Thank you for being my person. Thank you for listening to my endless talks. Thank you for making me feel special. Thank you for coming into my life.

I don't want a perfect love story. I just want OUR story, with all its silly moments, unexpected trips, hugs, fights, laughter and memories.

If I could go back and live those moments again, I would still want you beside me.

I Love You, My Baby! 💗

Today, tomorrow and in every little lifetime.

Forever yours,
